About Us

Wood that works — built for the way you live

Wood & Things is a joinery and woodwork workshop focused on practical, well-finished pieces for homes and businesses — from full kitchens and bedroom cupboards to TV units, vanities, shop fitting and set building.

We combine traditional craft with CNC fabrication so measurements stay true and details stay sharp. Whether it’s a new install or a refurbishment, the goal is the same: solid work that fits the space and lasts.

Based at Verona Park, Khaya Sands · Serving Johannesburg and surrounds
